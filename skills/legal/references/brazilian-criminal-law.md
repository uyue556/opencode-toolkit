## 2. Maria da Penha — violence forms, protective measures, victim flow

### Forms of violence (Art. 7, Lei 11.340/2006)

| Form | Definition | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Física** (I) | Harm to bodily integrity/health | Slaps, punches, shoves, burns, strangulation |
| **Psicológica** (II) | Emotional damage, lowered self-esteem, control | Humiliation, threats, isolation, gaslighting, manipulation |
| **Sexual** (III) | Constraint to witness/keep/participate in sex | Marital rape, blocking contraception, forcing abortion |
| **Patrimonial** (IV) | Retention, subtraction, destruction of objects | Holding documents, destroying phone, controlling money |
| **Moral** (V) | Calúnia, difamação, injúria | Public cheating accusations, exposing intimacy, insults |

### Protective measures — against the aggressor (Art. 22)

| # | Measure |
|---|---|
| I | Suspension of weapon possession/carry |
| II | Removal from the home/domicile |
| III-a | Prohibition of approach (minimum distance fixed by judge) |
| III-b | Prohibition of contact by any means |
| III-c | Prohibition of frequenting certain places |
| IV | Restriction/suspension of visits to minors |
| V | Provisional alimony |
| §5 | **Electronic monitoring** (tornozeleira) — Lei 15.125/2025 |

In favor of the victim (Art. 23): referral to protection program; return home after aggressor removed;
removal without loss of rights; separation of bodies. Patrimonial (Art. 24): restitution of taken goods;
no sale/lease of common goods; suspension of powers of attorney; provisional bond.

### Breaching a protective measure (Art. 24-A)

- Crime: failing to comply with a judicial order granting a protective measure.
- Current penalty: **reclusão 2–5 years + fine** (Lei 14.994/2024); previously detenção 3m–2y (Lei 13.641/2018).
- Public unconditional action; only the judge may grant bail; formal crime (breaching the order is enough).

### STJ súmulas on Maria da Penha

| Súmula | Content |
|---|---|
| 536 | Suspensão do processo (Art. 89, Lei 9.099) does not apply |
| 542 | Lesão corporal in domestic violence = public unconditional action |
| 588 | No substitution by restrictive-rights penalty |
| 589 | Princípio da insignificância inapplicable |
| 600 | Cohabitation is not required |

### Victim flow

```
VÍTIMA
├─ EMERGÊNCIA (immediate risk) → 190 (PM) or 180 (Central da Mulher)
│   └ flagrante + immediate removal → B.O./DEAM → protective measure ≤ 48h (Art. 12-C)
├─ URGÊNCIA (recurring) → B.O./DEAM → request measures → judge decides ≤48h (inaudita altera pars)
├─ ORIENTAÇÃO → CRAM / Defensoria Pública / OAB
└─ SINAL VERMELHO (Lei 14.188/2021) → draw red X on hand → show at pharmacy/hospital → establishment alerts authorities
```

Help channels: **180** (Central da Mulher, 24h), **190** (PM), **192** (SAMU), DEAM, Defensoria (129),
CRAM, Casa da Mulher Brasileira, online B.O. (varies by state).

## 3. Feminicídio (Art. 121-A CP)

- **Autonomous crime since Lei 14.994/2024** (before: qualificadora of homicídio, Art. 121 §2 VI).
- Text: "Matar mulher por razões da condição de sexo feminino — pena: reclusão 20 a 40 anos."
- Reasons of female condition (I domestic/family violence; II contempt/discrimination of woman).
- Causes of increase (up to 1/3): during pregnancy or up to 3 months post-partum; victim is mother/
  guardian of a child or disabled person; in presence of the victim's descendant/ascendant; while in
  breach of a protective measure. **Max penalty: up to 53y 4m.**
- Characteristics: qualificadora is **objective** (STJ); hediondo; initial regime fechado; progressão
  50/60/70%; jury; no bail; no indulto/graça/anistia.

## 5. Litigância de má-fé & ardilosidade

- **Conducts (Art. 80 CPC):** I claim/defense against express law or uncontested fact; II altering the
  truth; III using process for illegal goal; IV unjustified resistance; V temerity; VI manifestly
  unfounded incident; VII dilatory appeal.
- **Sanctions (Art. 81 CPC):** fine >1% and <10% of corrected case value + perdas e danos +
  attorney fees + costs.
- **Criminal process divergence:** STJ — no má-fé fine in criminal process (no CPP provision; analogy
  in malam partem forbidden); STF — fine possible on abuse of right to appeal. Present both currents.
- **Fraude processual (Art. 347 CP):** innovating the state of place/thing/person to mislead judge or
  expert — detenção 3m–2y + fine; doubled if aimed at criminal process.
- Related crimes: denunciação caluniosa (Art. 339, 2–8y), comunicação falsa (Art. 340), auto-acusação
  falsa (Art. 341), falso testemunho (Art. 342, 2–4y), coação no processo (Art. 344), exercício
  arbitrário (Art. 345), favorecimento pessoal/real (Art. 348/349).
- **Defense against an ardilosa accusation:** criminal complaint + indenização (Art. 339 CP + Art. 953
  CC), representation + evidence for false B.O. (Art. 340), contraditório + acareação for false witness,
  official contraperícia for forged laudo (Art. 182 CPP), revogação/HC for undue protective measure
  (Art. 19 §3 Lei 11.340). In Maria da Penha context, alleging falsity needs **robust proof** and risks
  revictimization — never trivialize the victim's word (which has special weight in clandestine crimes).

## 6. Dosimetria (triphasic system)

```
FASE 1 — Pena-base (Art. 59: culpabilidade, antecedentes, conduta social, personalidade,
          motivos, circunstâncias, consequências, comportamento da vítima)
FASE 2 — Agravantes (61–62) e atenuantes (65–66); cannot exceed legal limits (Súm. 231 STJ)
FASE 3 — Causas de aumento/diminuição; CAN exceed legal limits
```

Agravantes (Art. 61): reincidência (I); motivo fútil/torpe (II-a); traição/emboscada/dissimulação
(II-c); meio cruel (II-d); contra ascendente/descendente/cônjuge (II-e); abuso de autoridade (II-f/g);
against child/elder/sick/pregnant (II-h); breaching protective measure.

Regimes: fechado > 8y (or reincident > 4y); semiaberto 4–8y; aberto ≤ 4y; hediondo always fechado.

Progressão (Lei 13.964/2019): common 16/20%; with violence 25/30%; hediondo without death 40/50/60%;
**hediondo with death 50/60/70%**; organized crime command 50/60/70%.

## 7. Prescription & imprisonment

- Prescription (Art. 109 CP): <1y→3y; 1–2y→4y; 2–4y→8y; 4–8y→12y; 8–12y→16y; >12y→20y.
- Imprescriptible: racismo (Art. 5 XLII CF), armed groups against the State (Art. 5 XLIV CF).
  Feminicídio is NOT imprescriptible (20y).
- Suspension causes: prejudicial question, parliamentary, sursis, edictal citation. Interruption:
  receiving denúncia, pronúncia, sentencing, reincidence, start/continue of serving.
- Imprisonment types: flagrante (301–310 CPP); preventiva (311–316) — Art. 313 III for guaranteeing
  protective measures; temporária (Lei 7.960/89); definitiva. Custody hearing mandatory in 24h (Art. 310).
- Habeas corpus hypotheses: Art. 648 CPP I–VII.

## 8. Defense & prosecution theses

Defense theses for domestic violence: legítima defesa (Art. 25 — low viability), ausência de dolo
(medium), desclassificação lesão→vias de fato (medium), atipicidade (low — Súm. 589 bars insignificância),
retratação (Art. 16 — limited, conditional actions only), nulidade processual (medium), insuficiência
probatória / in dubio pro reo (high).

Prosecution theses: victim's word as evidence (consolidated STJ — clandestine crimes), contexto de
dominação (Art. 5 Lei 11.340), histórico de violência, laudos periciais (IML/psicológico),
descumprimento reiterado (Art. 24-A).

General defense theses: legítima defesa (25), estado de necessidade (24), estrito cumprimento do dever
legal / exercício regular de direito (23, III), erro de tipo (20), erro de proibição (21), coação
irresistível / obediência hierárquica (22), inimputabilidade (26), embriaguez involuntária completa
(28 §1), arrependimento posterior (16), crime impossível (17), desistência voluntária (15), prescrição (109).

## 9. 10-step criminal case workflow

1. **Enquadramento** — type (which article?), classification (doloso/culposo, tentado/consumado, hediondo).
2. **Sujeitos** — active/passive subject, relationship, victim vulnerability.
3. **Materialidade e autoria** — laudo, B.O., photos, witnesses, cameras, confession.
4. **Circunstâncias** — agravantes/atenuantes, causes of increase/decrease, concurso de crimes.
5. **Dosimetria estimada** — 3 phases + probable regime.
6. **Questões processuais** — competence, ação penal type, prison/cautelares.
7. **Teses disponíveis** — for defense and prosecution.
8. **Riscos e cenários** — optimistic/base/pessimistic.
9. **Estratégia** — ANPP if viable (Art. 28-A), defense at trial, negotiation, appeals.
10. **Veredicto técnico** — template: crime, base legal, estimated dosimetria, prescrição, most-likely
    scenario, risk level, recommendation, observations.

## 10. ANPP, property crimes, drug crimes, execution benefits

### ANPP (Art. 28-A CPP, Lei 13.964/2019)

Cumulative requirements: formal + circumstantial confession; minimum penalty < 4 years; **no violence or
grave threat (bars domestic violence)**; not a dismissal case; transação penal not available.
Impediments: reincident; ANPP/transação/sursis beneficiary in last 5y; criminal-habit indicators;
**domestic violence crimes**. Adjustable conditions (§1 I–V): reparation, renunciation, community
service, pecuniary payment, other MP conditions. Impact: no criminal antecedents, not a conviction;
breach → denúncia; full compliance → punibilidade extinta.

### Drugs

Uso (Art. 28) vs Tráfico (Art. 33): penalties, prison, fiança, sursis, liberdade provisória
(STF HC 104.339). Tráfico privilegiado (Art. 33 §4): primário + bons antecedentes + not part of
criminal organization → redução 1/6–2/3; NOT hediondo (STF HC 118.533). Associação (Art. 35): 2+ people;
organização criminosa needs 4+ (Lei 12.850/2013).

### Execution benefits

Progressão (16/20/25/30/40/50/60/70% per table above); livramento condicional 1/3 (1/2 reincident,
2/3 hediondo); saída temporária 1/6; trabalho externo 1/6; remição (3 days work = 1 day; 12h study =
1 day; reading — Recomendação 44/2013 CNJ: 1 book/30d = 4 days, max 12/year). Detração (Art. 42 CP).

## 11. Absolute restrictions

- Never invent laws, articles, súmulas or decisions; never guarantee a trial result.
- Never minimize domestic violence or blame the victim; never advise evidence destruction/obstruction.
- Always recommend a licensed criminal lawyer when a real case is at stake.
- When jurisprudence diverges, present both currents; flag when analysis depends on documents not provided.
