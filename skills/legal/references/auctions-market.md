|---|---|---|---|---|
| Apart. 1–2 quartos | 30–60d | 60–90d | 90–180d | 180–360d |
| Apart. 3 quartos | 60–90d | 90–150d | 120–240d | 240+d |
| Casa condomínio | 60–120d | 90–180d | 120–240d | 240+d |
| Sala comercial | 120–240d | 180–360d | 360+d | 360+d |

Accelerators: price 10–15% below market; renovated/presentable; regularized documentation; good photos
on ZAP/Viva Real; CRECI broker with client base. Brakes: pending documentation (ITBI, matrícula);
bad state/unfinished works; unpaid debts on the matrícula; pending litigation (ação real).

## 2. By auction modality

- **Judicial (CPC):** 1st auction (min = valuation): arrematação frequency 20–30%, avg deságio 0–15%.
  2nd (no min / vil preço barred): frequency 50–70%, avg deságio 30–50%, observed max up to 65–70% for
  problematic properties.
- **Extrajudicial (Lei 9.514 — banks):** 1st (min = contract value): frequency 30–50%, deságio 20–35%
  (CEF ~28% historical). 2nd (min = saldo devedor): frequency 60–80%, deságio 35–55% — saldo devedor
  can be far below market (opportunity).
- **Direct bank sale:** no competition; deságio 15–30%; financing by the bank possible; CEF finances up
  to 80% of evaluation value.

## 3. Deságio map by property situation

Vacant, no debts, docs ok: 15–25% · Vacant, quantified debts: 25–35% · Occupied (cooperative debtor):
30–40% · Occupied (litigious) + debts: 40–55% · Documentally irregular: 35–50% · Bad state: 35–55% ·
Combination of problems: 50–70%.

## 4. Exit strategies A–D

- **A — Flip rápido (3–12 months, medium risk):** buy ≥35% deságio → regularize docs (1–3m) → light
  reform if needed → sell 15–20% below VMP → gross profit 15–25%. Capital = 100% of bid + costs.
- **B — Reforma & valorização (6–18m, med-high risk):** buy ≥40% deságio → full reform (3–6m) → sell at
  renovated-market premium (20–30%) → gross 30–50%. Capital = 100% bid + 20–30% of bid in works.
- **C — Renda (long term, passive income):** buy ≥25% deságio → regularize and rent (1–3m) → rent below
  market for speed → yield above market via lower cost basis. Yields: normal market 4–6% a.a.; at 30%
  deságio 6–9%; at 40% deságio 7–12%.
- **D — Regularização & revenda (expert):** buy problem properties ≥50% deságio → resolve legal/
  documentary issues → sell regularized at market → gross 40–70%. For lawyers/specialists.

## 5. Quick ROI simulation & benchmarks

```
DADOS: VAN R$__ · VMP R$__ · Lance R$__ · Deságio over VMP __%
CUSTOS: comissão 5% + ITBI 3% + registro/escritura + advogado + débitos + obras = CUSTO TOTAL
SAÍDA: valor de venda esperado − corretagem 5–6% − IRPF 15% ganho de capital
RESULTADO: lucro bruto/líquido, ROI bruto/líquido, prazo, retorno anualizado
```

Benchmarks (ref. 2024–25): CDI ~10.5–13.15% a.a.; IPCA ~4.5–5%; LCI/LCA isentas 9–12%; FIIs yield
~9–12%. **To beat CDI you need annualized ROI ≥ 15–20%.** Minimum deságio to clear CDI (considering
~10% acquisition costs): **~25% over VMP.**

## 6. Best time to buy & seasonality

- **High rates (Selic >12%):** more default → more auctions → less competition → best time to buy.
- **Low rates (Selic <9%):** fewer auctions, more competition, higher prices.
- Seasonality: December/January low competition (holidays); March–April Caixa new lots; July low
  competition; October–November judicial-auction high season (year-end process).
- Ref. indicators (fev/2025): Selic 13.25%, CDI ~13.15%, IPCA ~5%, IGP-M ~4.5%, USD/BRL ~5.80–6.00,
  poupança ~7.7% (Selic>8.5%), LCI/LCA 10–12%, FIIs ~10–12%. High Selic = favorable moment (more
  supply, less competition).

## 7. By bank & buyer profile

- **CEF:** biggest inventory (>20k in 2024); Venda Online/Licitação Aberta/Proposta Online; strong in
  MCMV popular; finances up to 80%; FGTS allowed.
- **Santander (santanderx.com.br):** medium stock, mid-high standard, monthly auctions.
- **Itaú/Bradesco/BB:** smaller stocks, all standards; extrajudicial more frequent; tend to clear stock
  in December.

**End-buyer profile mapping** speeds exit: middle-class family (apt 3Q / casa condomínio → ZAP/VivaReal,
corretor); young couple (studio/1–2Q central → Instagram, Quinto Andar); entrepreneur/investor
(comercial/galpão/terreno → CRECI); locador (located apt/studio → specialized agencies); incorporador
(terreno ZEU/ZC → brokers); FII/REIT (galpão/laje corporativa/varejo → B3, gestores).

## 8. Exit risks & weekly monitoring

Risks: local market drop (mitigate: geographic diversification); not renting/selling on time (accept
bigger exit discount); renovation over budget (30% works margin); new competing development (check
alvarás); negative zoning change (check plano diretor); economic slowdown (favor basic-needs assets);
sudden Selic rise (favor quick flip).

Weekly routine: set alerts (ZAP, Viva Real, CEF, TJ per comarca); analyze each new lot in 30 min
(edital → comparables → Street View → ROI → certidão de ônus if interesting); on-site diligence if
ROI >20%; final decision = edital risk score + net ROI vs CDI + capital + timeline → max bid → enter.

## 9. Financing & CDI benchmark

Post-auction financing example (CEF 2025): R$300k / 360m / 11.5% a.a. → initial installment ~R$3,450,
total ~R$700k over 30y. **Rule of thumb: only finance if deságio >30% AND rate <12% a.a.; paying cash
always wins if you have capital.**

CDI benchmark worked example: R$500k; CDI net (~11.2% after 15% IR) = R$56k/yr. To beat it in 12
months need >R$56k net, i.e. ROI >11.2% a.a., i.e. **~25% deságio over VMP** after costs.

Investment comparison: CDI/Tesouro 11–13% a.a. (very low risk, D+0–1); FIIs 10–12% (medium, D+2);
LCI/LCA 10–12% (low, 90d); direct buy 4–8% (medium, 3–12m); **Leilão-Flip 20–50%/period (med-high,
3–12m, R$50k+); Leilão-Renda 8–15% a.a. (medium, 12+m, R$100k+); Leilão-Reforma 30–60%/period (high,
6–18m, R$150k+)**.

Conclusion: auctions only consistently beat CDI with (1) ≥25–30% deságio, (2) full due diligence,
(3) exit strategy defined before bidding, (4) 15–20% reserve for surprises.
