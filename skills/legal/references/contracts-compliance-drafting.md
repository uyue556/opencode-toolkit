# Contract & Compliance Document Drafting

Merged from `legal-advisor` (privacy/ToS/compliance documents) and `employment-contract-templates`
(employment docs). Use `employment-contract-playbook.md` for full employment templates and
`lex-cross-jurisdictional.md` for US/CA/EU jurisdiction nuances.

## When to use

Drafting privacy policies, terms of service, user agreements, cookie policies, DPAs, disclaimers,
liability limitations, IP notices, SaaS/licensing terms, e-commerce legal requirements, email-marketing
compliance (CAN-SPAM, CASL), age verification/children's privacy (COPPA), employment contracts, offer
letters, handbooks, HR policies, NDAs, non-competes, onboarding documentation.

## Do not use when

- Jurisdiction-specific legal advice is required (no licensed counsel available in scope).
- The request is unrelated to legal/compliance documentation.

## Instructions

1. Clarify goals, constraints, jurisdiction(s), and required inputs (business model, audience, data flows).
2. Identify applicable jurisdictions and regulations.
3. Choose the document structure; tailor role-specific terms (compensation, benefits, contingencies).
4. Validate compensation/benefits/compliance; add signature, confidentiality, and IP-assignment terms as needed.
5. Use clear, accessible language while maintaining legal precision; include all mandatory disclosures.
6. Provide options for different business models; flag areas requiring specific legal review.
7. Include an update-tracking note for regulatory changes.

## Focus areas

- Privacy policies (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA, DPA-UK)
- Terms of service and user agreements
- Cookie policies and consent management
- Data processing agreements (DPA)
- Disclaimers and liability limitations
- Intellectual property notices
- SaaS/software licensing terms
- E-commerce legal requirements
- Email marketing compliance (CAN-SPAM, CASL)
- Age verification and children's privacy (COPPA)
- Employment: contracts, offer letters, handbooks, HR policies, NDAs, non-competes

## Key regulations

GDPR (EU), CCPA/CPRA (California), LGPD (Brazil), PIPEDA (Canada), Data Protection Act (UK), COPPA
(children), CAN-SPAM (email), ePrivacy Directive (cookies), plus employment law (at-will vs statutory
notice — see lex reference).

## Output checklist

- Complete document with proper structure and logical headers
- Jurisdiction-specific variations where needed
- Placeholder sections for company-specific info
- Implementation notes for technical requirements
- Compliance checklist per regulation
- Update tracking for regulatory changes
- **Mandatory disclaimer:** "This is a template for informational purposes. Consult with a qualified
  attorney for legal advice specific to your situation."

## Employment docs — core considerations

- Employment relationship choices: at-will vs contract; employee vs contractor; full-time vs part-time;
  exempt vs non-exempt; jurisdiction-specific requirements.
- Document types: offer letter (pre-hire), employment contract (hire), handbook (onboarding), NDA
  (before access), non-compete (hire/exit).
- Safety: templates are not legal advice; consult qualified counsel before use; non-competes vary
  strongly by jurisdiction (see lex reference — some states ban them, EU often requires garden leave or
  mandatory compensation).

## Safety

- Never present templates as legal advice; always recommend licensed counsel for a real deployment.
- Keep disclaimers visible; do not silently drop them.
