## 1. Divorce

### Consensual extrajudicial (Lei 11.441/2007)

- Both agree; **no minor/incapable children** (else judicial); public deed at a notary; lawyer
  mandatory (one for both is allowed); immediate (no separation period since EC 66/2010); typical cost
  R$ 1.500–4.000; partilha may be included in the same deed.

### Judicial divorce (Art. 731–734 CPC)

- Consensual: homologated by judge (Art. 731). Litigioso: judge decides (Art. 693).
- Competence: domicile of the child's guardian or last couple's domicile (Art. 53, I CPC).

### Property regimes (Art. 1.639–1.688 CC)

| Regime | Characteristic | Meação |
|---|---|---|
| Comunhão parcial (default) | Acquired during marriage = common | 50% of aquestos |
| Comunhão universal | Everything common (exceptions Art. 1.668) | 50% of all |
| Separação total | Nothing common | none |
| Separação obrigatória | Imposed by law (Art. 1.641) | Súmula 377 STF: aquestos are meados |
| Participação final nos aquestos | Separate during, common at dissolution | 50% of valorization |

Súmula 377 STF: in mandatory separation, goods acquired during marriage communicate. Applies to
marriages of 70+ year-olds (Art. 1.641 II CC) — spouse still gets meação of goods acquired during the union.

## 2. Alimentos (support)

- Bases: Art. 1.694–1.710 CC; Lei 5.478/1968 (special proceeding); Art. 528–533 CPC (execução).
- Types: provisórios (liminar), provisionais (tutela de urgência), definitivos, compensatórios
  (REsp 1.954.279), gravídicos (Lei 11.804/2008), transitivos.
- Execução options: prisão civil (Art. 528 §3, 1–3m closed regime, last 3 prestações — Súmula 309 STJ);
  penhora; desconto em folha (Art. 529, up to 50% net); SISBAJUD; protesto.
- Binômio necessidade × possibilidade (Art. 1.694 §1). Common parameters: 1 child ~30% net (CLT),
  2 children 33–40%, 3+ 40–50%; autonomous/informal: 1–3 salários mínimos; ex-spouse 20–33% (temporary).

## 3. Stable union & paternity

- Stable union (Art. 1.723–1.727): public, continuous, durable cohabitation with family intent; partial
  communion by default; recognition judicial/extrajudicial/post-mortem. Succession: STF RE 878.694
  (Tema 498) equated companion to spouse — Art. 1.790 CC declared unconstitutional; Art. 1.829 applies.
- Paternity (Lei 8.560/1992; Art. 1.606–1.617 CC): DNA refusal creates presumption (Súmula 301 STJ);
  imprescriptible (Art. 27 ECA); negatória (Art. 1.601 CC); socio-afetiva doesn't block biological
  (STF Tema 622).

## 4. Asset division in divorce

**Comunicam** (partial communion): properties bought during marriage, vehicles, investments from work
income, joint-account balance, FGTS accrued during the union (STJ), previdência privada (divergent).
**Não comunicam** (particular): goods prior to marriage, inheritance/donation received (Art. 1.659 I),
sub-rogated goods, goods with incomunicabilidade clause, personal-use goods/books/profession tools,
income from personal labor (controversial).

Valuation methods: pericial (Art. 464 CPC), party agreement, market appraisal (corretor/avaliador),
patrimonial balance (quotas).

## 5. Inventory & succession

### Extrajudicial inventory (Art. 610 §1 CPC + Lei 11.441/2007)

All heirs of age and capable; consensus on partilha; no will (exception: confirmed will per Resolução
CNJ 35/2007 Art. 12-A); public deed at any comarca; lawyer mandatory; 60-day deadline (Art. 611 CPC,
ITCMD penalty if exceeded).

### Judicial inventory (Art. 610–673 CPC)

Arrolamento sumário (Art. 659, capable heirs + agreement); arrolamento comum (Art. 664, up to 1.000 SM);
traditional (incapable heirs, divergence, will).

### Order of vocation (Art. 1.829 CC)

1) Descendants + spouse (spouse concurs — Art. 1.832); 2) Ascendants + spouse (spouse gets 1/3 with
father and mother — Art. 1.837); 3) Surviving spouse alone; 4) Collaterals to 4th degree.

Spouse concurrence by regime: partial communion → concurs on falecido's **particular** goods;
universal → doesn't concur; mandatory separation → doesn't (controversial, Súm. 377); conventional
separation → concurs on everything (REsp 1.382.170).

## 6. Wills & ITCMD

- Will types: público (Art. 1.864, tabelião + 2 witnesses), cerrado (Art. 1.868), particular
  (Art. 1.876, +3 witnesses), codicilo (Art. 1.881).
- Legítima (Art. 1.846 CC): 50% of the estate reserved for necessary heirs (descendants, ascendants,
  spouse); the testator freely disposes only of the other half.
- ITCMD: event = death transmission or donation; rate 1–8% by state (CF Art. 155 §1 IV); competence =
  state of deceased's domicile; 60-day deadline (progressive fine if exceeded).

### Sobrepartilha (Art. 669 CPC)

For sonegado goods, goods discovered after partilha, litigious/difficult-liquidation goods, remote goods.

## 7. Custody, visitation, parental alienation

### Types of custody (Art. 1.583–1.590 CC + Lei 13.058/2014)

Compartilhada (REGRA, even without consensus — Art. 1.584 §2); unilateral (Art. 1.583 §1); alternada
(jurisprudência); nidação (rare). Shared custody does NOT exclude alimentos (REsp 1.629.994).

### Visitation (Art. 1.589 CC)

Right of the parent AND the child (child's interest prevails); grandparents have visitation rights
(§ único, Lei 12.398/2011); non-compliance → busca e apreensão (Art. 461 CPC) + fine; supervised visits
when risk.

### Parental alienation (Lei 12.318/2010)

- Definition (Art. 2): interference in the child's psychological formation to harm the bond with the
  other parent. Forms (Art. 2 § único I–VII): disparaging campaigns, hindering parental authority,
  hindering contact, hindering coexistence rights, omitting relevant info, false accusations, moving to
  hinder contact.
- Sanctions (Art. 6): warning → widening visitation → fine → psychological follow-up → custody change →
  suspension of parental authority (increasing gravity).

### Modification of custody (Art. 1.586 CC)

When circumstances change, child's interest is harmed, proven alienation, physical/psychological risk,
or the adolescent's wish (Art. 12 ECA).

**Súmulas relevantes:** STJ 301 (DNA refusal), 309 (civil prison = last 3 months), 336 (alimentos from
citation), 364 (bem de família protects single/separated/widowed), 596 (transitivos). SV 25 STF (civil
prison only for alimony debtor).
