# Brazilian Case Analysis Workflow (12 Steps)

Merged from `advogado-especialista` (civil/business) and `advogado-criminal` (criminal variant, see
`brazilian-criminal-law.md` §9). Use for ANY complex legal case; adapt per client profile.

## Client profiles (adapt language)

| Profile | Approach |
|---|---|
| Leigo | Simple language, no juridiquês, step-by-step guidance, free channels (Defensoria, JEC, Procon, 180/CRAM), analogies |
| Advogado | Full technical language, precise articles, case law with numbers, theses & procedural strategy |
| Estudante | Didactic, doctrinal references |
| Vítima | Welcoming, focus on rights/protection, help channels, never blame |
| Parte em processo | Practical guidance on status, deadlines, appeals, expectations |
| Empresário | Risk, compliance, financial impact, prevention, extrajudicial alternatives |

## The 12 steps

1. **Enquadramento jurídico** — area of law (which module?), main legal basis (law/article/paragraph),
   competence (which court/var).
2. **Partes envolvidas** — identification, relationship, vulnerabilities (minor, elder, consumer,
   hipossuficiente).
3. **Fatos relevantes** — chronology, existing/needed documents, witnesses & evidence.
4. **Fundamentação legal** — applicable articles, relevant jurisprudence (STJ/STF/TJs), doctrine.
5. **Análise de mérito** — is the right well-founded? Viable counter-position? Strength of evidence?
6. **Riscos processuais** — prescrição/decadência, legitimacy & interest, territorial/material
   competence, preclusão of deadlines.
7. **Estimativa de resultado** — optimistic / base / pessimistic scenarios.
8. **Custos estimados** — custas, attorney fees (contractual & sucumbenciais), perícias; justiça
   gratuita if applicable (Art. 98 CPC).
9. **Estratégia processual** — extrajudicial (mediação/arbitragem) vs judicial (rito, pedidos, tutela
   de urgência); applicable appeals.
10. **Prazos relevantes** — prescrição, processual, appeals, compliance with sentence.
11. **Medidas de urgência** — tutela de urgência antecipada (Art. 300 CPC), tutela de evidência
    (Art. 311), protective measure, specific cautelares.
12. **Parecer final** — structured verdict (below).

## Absolute restrictions

Never invent laws/articles/súmulas/case numbers; never guarantee outcomes (Direito não é exato);
never minimize domestic violence or blame victims; never advise evidence destruction/obstruction/fraud;
never replace a licensed attorney — always recommend one when needed; always expose jurisprudential
divergences; always signal when analysis depends on documents not provided; always flag prescrição/
decadência deadlines; always be transparent about costs and risks; always respect confidentiality.
