## 1. Before Creating Content

Gather: **Goals** (awareness/leads/traffic/community; personal vs. company brand), **Audience** (who, platforms, what they engage with, problems), **Brand voice** (tone, topics to avoid, style), **Resources** (time, existing content to repurpose, video capacity, customer stories/data).

## 2. Platform Strategy Guide

### LinkedIn — best for B2B/thought leadership
- Frequency 3–5x/wk; best Tue–Thu 7–8am, 12pm, 5–6pm.
- Works: personal stories with business lessons, contrarian takes, behind-the-scenes, original data, carousels, polls.
- Format: first line is everything; 1,200–1,500 chars; **links in comments, not post body**; line breaks; tag sparingly.
- Algorithm: first-hour engagement matters; comments > reactions > clicks; dwell time signals quality; no external links in body.

### Twitter/X — tech, real-time, community
- 3–10 posts/day incl. replies. Tweets <100 chars engage more; threads: hook → promise → deliver; quote tweets beat plain retweets; visuals stop the scroll.

### Instagram — visual, e-commerce, younger
- 1–2 feed + 3–10 stories/day; best 11am–1pm, 7–9pm.
- Reels get ~2x reach of static; first frame must hook; carousels: up to 10 slides of value; use all story features (polls, questions).
- Algorithm: saves & shares > likes; consistency > perfection.

### TikTok — awareness, 16–34
- 1–4x/day. Hook in first 1–2 sec; keep <30 sec to start; vertical only (9:16); native/unpolished; trending sounds/formats; duets/stitches; post consistently.

### Facebook — communities, local, older
- 1–2x/day; best 1–4pm weekdays. Groups, native/live video, local content. **External links kill reach.**

## 3. Content Pillars

Build around 3–5 pillars. Example for a SaaS founder: Industry insights 30% · Behind-the-scenes 25% · Educational 25% · Personal 15% · Promotional 5%.

For each pillar ask: What unique perspective? What questions does the audience ask? What performed before? What can you produce consistently? What aligns with goals?

---

## 4. Hook Formulas

The first line decides whether anyone reads the rest.

- **Curiosity:** "I was wrong about [common belief]." / "The real reason [outcome] happens isn't what you think."
- **Story:** "Last week, [unexpected thing] happened." / "3 years ago I [past]. Today I [present]."
- **Value:** "How to [outcome] (without [pain])." / "The simplest way to [outcome]."
- **Contrarian:** "Unpopular opinion: [bold statement]" / "[Common advice] is wrong. Here's why."
- **Social proof:** "We [achieved result] in [timeframe]. Here's how."

## 5. Post Templates

### LinkedIn story post
```
[Hook: unexpected outcome or lesson]
[Set the scene]
[The challenge]
[What you tried / what happened]
[The turning point]
[The result]
[The lesson for readers]
[Question to prompt engagement]
```

### LinkedIn list/how-to
```
[X things I learned about [topic] after [credibility builder]:
1. [Point] — [Brief explanation]
2. …
[Wrap-up insight]
Which resonates most with you?
```
How-to: `How to [outcome] in [timeframe]:` → Step + why each matters → result you can expect → CTA/question.

### Twitter thread (tutorial)
Tweet 1: hook + promise ("Here's exactly how to [outcome] (step-by-step):") → one step per tweet → final: summary + follow CTA.

### Instagram carousel
Slide 1 bold statement → slides 2–9 one point each → slide 10 summary + CTA. Caption expands + CTA.

### Reel script
Hook (0–2s pattern interrupt) → Setup (2–5s context) → Value (5–25s advice) → CTA (25–30s).

## 6. Repurposing System

Turn one piece of pillar content into many: **Blog post** → LinkedIn insight (link in comments), LinkedIn carousel of main points, X thread of takeaways, single hot-take tweet, Instagram carousel + Reel. **Podcast/video** → quote graphics, quote threads, Reels/Shorts/TikTok clips.

Workflow: create pillar → extract 3–5 insights → adapt per platform → schedule across the week → reshare evergreen.

## 7. Content Calendar & Scheduling

- Weekly mix example: Mon industry insight, Tue behind-scenes, Wed educational, Thu story post, Fri hot take; leave weekends for curated/engagement.
- Monthly: Week 1 launch/announce, Week 2 educational deep-dive, Week 3 community focus, Week 4 story/behind-scenes.
- **Schedule** core content, threads, carousels, evergreen. **Post live** real-time commentary, responses, engagement.
- Maintain 1–2 weeks of scheduled content; review weekly; leave gaps for spontaneous posts.

## 8. Engagement & Analytics

**Daily routine (30 min):** respond to comments → comment on 5–10 target accounts (add insight, not "Great post!") → share with added insight → 2–3 DMs to new connections.

**Metrics:** Awareness = impressions/reach/follower growth. Engagement = engagement rate, comments > likes, shares, saves. Conversion = link clicks, profile visits, DMs, attributed leads.

**Weekly review:** top/bottom 3 posts (why?), follower growth trend, engagement trend, best posting times, pillar performance. If engagement low → test hooks/times/formats. If reach declining → no external links in body, post more, engage more, try video.

**Negative comments:** respond calmly, don't get defensive, take legitimate criticism offline, block/mute trolls.

## 9. Reverse-Engineering Viral Content

1. **Niche ID** — find 10–20 creators (consistent, high engagement vs. followers, audience overlap, mix of established + rising).
2. **Scrape** — collect 500–1,000+ posts (Apify, PhantomBuster, manual): text, engagement, format, timing, hook, CTA, topic.
3. **Analyze** — rank by engagement rate; find top 10%; extract hook/format/CTA patterns.
4. **Playbook** — codify patterns, e.g. "I [unexpected action] and [surprising result]" (curiosity + contrarian); "[Specific number] [things] that [outcome]:" (specificity + loss aversion); "[Controversial take]" (pattern interrupt).
5. **Layer voice** — write like a smart friend who figured something out; **specific > vague** ($47,329 not "good revenue"; 2,847 people not "a lot"); short. Breathe. Land.; write from emotion.
6. **Convert** — soft conversions (newsletter in bio, "Comment X and I'll send you…", optimized profile CTA) and direct (link in comments, contextual product mentions, case-study posts).

## 10. LinkedIn Deep-Dive

### Post writing: pick formula by engagement goal first
| Goal | Earned by | Formulas |
|---|---|---|
| Comments | questions, contrarian, vulnerability | Time-Anchor Confession, Contrarian + Receipts, Permission Slip, Curiosity-Gap |
| Reposts | quotable maxims, tributes | Named Gratitude, R.I.P. Obituary, Paid-vs-Free Reversal |
| Likes | emotional stories, celebrations | Emotional Cold-Open, Bait-and-Switch, Status-Strip Humility |
| Saves | frameworks, exact how-tos | Explain-to-Kids, Odd-Precision Money Ledger, Paid-vs-Free Reversal |

2026 formatting rules: hook in first 210 chars (before "…see more"); 900–1,300 chars sweet spot; double line breaks; 0–2 hashtags at end; no external links in body; prefer "How I" over "How to"; one specific number in the first sentence ($873.47 beats $900).

**AI-tell scrub:** strip em dashes, AI vocabulary ("game-changer", "deep dive", "delve"), rule-of-three lists without receipts, generic openers. Add per 100 words: ≥1 specific number, ≥1 named entity, ≥1 first-person concrete detail; vary sentence length aggressively.

Never: blend two formulas, use all-caps openings, frame LinkedIn as inferior inside a LinkedIn post, or use F5 Self-Proving Meta unless the promise will be kept.

### Profile optimization
- **Headline:** Authority statement + value prop + keywords (not "Job Title at Company").
- **About:** hook → problem-solving → proof → CTA; primary keywords in first 2–3 lines; first person.
- **Featured:** best work, active links, descriptive titles.
- **Experience:** [Action Verb] [Metric/Task] to achieve [Impact/Result].
- **Skills:** merge low-scope skills into high-authority clusters (HTML+CSS+JS → Full-Stack); curate 10–15 strategic skills; remove "Teamwork/Office".
- **Brand:** find the "Red Thread" uniting roles; one primary anchor identity, secondary roles as supporting expertise.
- **CTA audit:** every profile should tell the visitor what to do next.

## 11. Chinese Platforms

### 小红书 (Xiaohongshu)
Search-first; algorithm prioritizes **saves** over likes; CTR driven by cover image + title.
- Formats: Before/After (妆前妆后), Step-by-Step (5步学会), Comparison (A vs B 实测), Hidden Gems, List/Rankings.
- Title: primary keyword in **first 8 chars**, emotional trigger, 18–22 chars optimal, numbers + specific results.
- Package: cover brief (text overlay <10 chars), 2–3 titles, body (hook → short paragraphs 2–3 sentences → strategic emoji → highlighted key info → CTA), 3–5 hashtags (mix high-volume + niche), comment plan (seed comments, anticipated Q&A).
- Sound like a person sharing a discovery, not a brand broadcasting. Avoid absolute claims; disclose sponsorships.

### 微信公众号 (WeChat Official Account)
- Account types: 订阅号 (1 push/day, folded) vs 服务号 (4 pushes/month, main chat list).
- Title formulas: Curiosity gap (为什么X却Y?), counter-intuitive (一直以为X，原来Y), social proof (X万人都在用), urgency (再不看就晚了!), value promise (看完这篇，你就懂了), identity (X的人，都有一个共性).
- Architecture: **首屏 first fold** (hook + value promise, must stand alone) → Ramp → Core content → **Emotional peak** (creates sharing) → CTA (follow/share/Mini-Program).
- Short paragraphs; 1 image per 300–500 words; peak posting 7–9am, 12–1pm, 8–10pm China time; craft Moments share-worthy pull quotes + suggested forwarding text.
