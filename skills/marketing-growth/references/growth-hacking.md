## 1. Growth Frameworks

**Pirate Metrics (AARRR):** Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Revenue → Referral.

For each stage define the metric, target, and channel:
- **Acquisition:** how people discover you (SEO, Product Hunt, influencers, PR, paid).
- **Activation:** when the user first experiences value (e.g., first conversation/report/result); track completion rate and time-to-first-value.
- **Retention:** do they return? (D7/D30/D90 targets; weekly active metrics).
- **Revenue:** conversion rate, MRR/ARPU/LTV.
- **Referral:** NPS, referrals per user, K-factor.

## 2. Launch Strategy

### Core philosophy
Launch again and again — every feature/update is a chance to capture attention. A strong launch compounds momentum through early user access, learning, and repeated splash moments.

### The ORB Framework (Owned / Rented / Borrowed)
Everything funnels back to **owned channels**:
- **Owned:** email list, blog, podcast, branded community, website. They compound over time and aren't subject to algorithms. Start with 1–2 based on audience.
- **Rented:** social platforms, app stores, YouTube, Reddit. Speed, not stability — use them to drive traffic to owned channels. Example: Notion hacked virality on Twitter/YouTube/Reddit but funneled all visibility into signups + email onboarding.
- **Borrowed:** guest content, podcasts, webinars, speaking, influencer partnerships — someone else's audience. Convert borrowed attention into owned relationships. Example: TRMNL sent a free e-ink display to a YouTuber → 500K views → $500K sales.

### Five-phase launch
1. **Internal:** one-on-one early testers, iron out issues, functional prototype for demos.
2. **Alpha:** landing page + early access form, announce existence, invite individually, MVP in production.
3. **Beta:** scale early access, teasers about the problem you solve, recruit friends/investors/influencers, waitlist.
4. **Early Access:** leak screenshots/demos, gather quantitative + qualitative data, run PMF survey, throttle invites in batches (5–10%).
5. **Full launch:** open self-serve signups, start charging, announce across all channels (email, in-app, website banner, blog, social, Product Hunt/BetaList/HN).

### Product Hunt
- **Before:** build relationships with influential supporters/communities; optimize listing (tagline, visuals, short demo video); study successful launches; engage communities with value first.
- **Launch day:** treat as an all-day event; respond to every comment in real-time; direct traffic to capture signups.
- **After:** follow up, convert PH traffic into email relationships, keep momentum with post-launch content.

### Post-launch
Automated onboarding email sequence · include announcement in roundup email · publish comparison pages · update site sections · offer no-code interactive demo · keep announcing regularly (major = full campaign, medium = targeted, minor = changelog) to signal active development.

### Launch checklist (pre/launch day/post) — see email & social references for the supporting sequences.

## 3. Referral & Affiliate Programs

### Referral vs Affiliate
| | Referral | Affiliate |
|---|---|---|
| Referrer | Existing customer | May not be customer |
| Motivation | Rewards + helping friends | Revenue/commission |
| Rewards | One-time/limited | Ongoing commission |
| Volume | Lower, higher trust | Higher, variable trust |
| Best for | Products with word-of-mouth, lower-ticket | Reaching new audiences, higher-ticket |

Hybrid: referral for customers (simple, small rewards) + affiliate for partners (structured commissions).

### The referral loop
Trigger moment → Share action → Referred convert → Reward (→ new trigger).

**Trigger moments:** after aha moment, milestone, exceptional support, renewal/upgrade, explicit praise. Natural sharing: collaboration, "what tool do you use?", shareable results.

**Share mechanisms (by effectiveness):** in-product sharing > personalized link > email invitation > social sharing > referral code. Offer multiple; lead with highest-converting.

**Incentive structures:** single-sided (simpler; referred may lack urgency) · double-sided (higher conversion; standard) · tiered (gamified, more complex).
Incentive types: cash/credit (universal, transactional) · product credit (drives usage) · free months (clear value) · feature unlock (low cost) · swag (memorable, logistics) · charity (feel-good, lower motivation).

**Incentive sizing:**
```
Max Reward = (Customer LTV × Gross Margin) − Target CAC
```
Typical: B2C $10–50 or 10–25% of first purchase · B2B SaaS $50–500 or 1–3 months free · Enterprise custom.

### Affiliate design
Commission: % of sale (10–30%) · flat fee per action ($5–500) · recurring (10–25% for 12 mo) · tiered (motivates high performers). **Cookie duration:** 24h impulse purchases · 7–14d e-commerce · 30d SaaS/B2B · 60–90d enterprise · lifetime premium.
**Recruitment:** existing content creators, bloggers/reviewers, YouTubers, newsletter writers, complementary tools, consultants. **Enablement kit:** tracking links, product overview, target audience, competitor comparison, creative assets, sample copy, case studies, demo access, FAQ, payment terms.

### Metrics
- **K-factor** = Invitations × Conversion Rate. >1 viral; <1 amplified.
- **Referral rate** = customers who refer ÷ total. Good 10–25%, great 25–50%.
- **Referrals per referrer:** avg 1–2, good 2–5.
- **ROI** = (Revenue from referred − program costs) ÷ program costs. Track referred CAC, referred LTV, payback.
- Referred customers typically: +16–25% LTV, −18–37% churn, 2–3x referral rate.

### Optimization
Low awareness → prominent in-app prompts. Low share → one-click sharing. Low conversion → optimize referred-user landing. Fraud → email verification, device fingerprinting, IP monitoring, delayed payout, minimum activity, caps, reward clawback, product-credit payouts. One-time referrers → tiered/gamified rewards.

### Launch sequence
Announce (reward + how it works) → nurture non-referrers (D7/D30/D60) → re-engage past referrers with success stories.

## 4. Viral Loops & Viral Coefficient

### Viral loop anatomy
Trigger (amazing experience / great result / shareable moment) → Action (share) → Referred user → Reward/next trigger.

Example loop designs: organic word-of-mouth (K target), share-a-great-result button ("Share this insight" → pre-made post), referral program (both sides rewarded).

### K-factor calculator
```
K = (% who invite) × (invitations per user) × (conversion rate)
K ≥ 1  viral (each user brings >1)
K ≥ 0.5 good (accelerated)
K ≥ 0.2 ok (supported)
K < 0.2 slow
```
Interpretation: "each 100 users bring K×100 new."

## 5. Free Tool Strategy (Engineering as Marketing)

### Core principles
1. **Solve a real problem** — useful even without your product.
2. **Adjacent to core product** — natural path to what you sell.
3. **Simple and focused** — one thing, low friction, immediate value.
4. **Worth the investment** — (lead value × expected leads) > build + maintenance cost.

### Tool types
Calculators (personalized output, share-worthy) · Generators (tangible output, saves time) · Analyzers/auditors (curiosity-driven, creates problem awareness) · Testers/validators (immediate utility, bookmark-worthy) · Libraries/resources (high SEO value, linkable) · Interactive educational (deep engagement, expertise).

### Ideation
Start from pain points: what does your audience Google? What's tedious manual work? What do they need before buying? What info do they wish they had?
**Validate:** search demand, uniqueness (10x better), lead quality (audience = buyers, natural path to product), build feasibility (MVP scope, maintenance).

### SEO
Tool pages target "[thing] calculator/generator", "free [tool type]", "[industry] [tool type]". Supporting content ("How to…", "What is…") links to the tool. Tools attract links naturally (useful, unique, shareable); pursue roundup posts, resource pages, industry publications. Fast load, mobile-friendly, crawlable (not JS-only), proper meta + schema.

### Lead capture
Fully gated (highest value; lower usage) · **partially gated — show preview, gate details (best balance, most common)** · ungated with optional capture (email to save/share) · fully ungated (pure SEO/brand play). Post-capture: immediate email with results, nurture sequence relevant to tool topic, clear path to product.

### Build vs buy vs embed
Build: unique, core to brand, have capacity. No-code: Outgrow/Involve.me, Typeform/Tally, Bubble/Webflow (speed, testing). Embed: something good exists, not a differentiator.

### MVP
Core functionality only + essential UX + basic lead capture. Skip accounts, saving results, advanced features. Iterate from usage data.

### Evaluation scorecard (1–5 each; 25+ strong, 15–24 promising, <15 reconsider)
Search demand · audience match · uniqueness · natural path to product · build feasibility · maintenance burden (inverse) · link-building potential · share-worthiness.

### ROI projection
`Monthly value = leads/mo × lead→customer% × avg customer value`. Payback = build cost ÷ (monthly value − maintenance).

## 6. Viral Generator / Quiz / Calculator Ideas

### Formula
```
Input (minimal) → Magic (algorithm) → Result (shareable)
```
Inputs ranked by virality: name-only / birthday / photo (low friction) > quiz answers (more investment).

### Result types that get shared
Identity ("You are a…") · comparison ("You're 87% like…") · prediction · score · visual (avatar/badge/certificate).

**The screenshot test:** result must look great as a screenshot; subtle branding; mobile-readable; design for screenshots even if you add share buttons.

### Quiz pattern
5–10 questions → weighted scoring → 4–8 distinct results (sweet spot; desirable, distinct, include "rare" results). Question types by engagement: image choice > this-or-that > slider > multiple choice > text. Result page: big bold title, flattering description, shareable card, share buttons, "see what friends got", subtle retake.

### Name generator pattern
Deterministic (same input = same output — shareable), seeded random, combinatorial (word parts), or AI-powered. Make results feel personal (use their input, add meaning/backstory, visual).

### Calculator virality
Topics: salary/money, age/time, compatibility, worth/value, predictions. Formula: interesting inputs → impressive calculation → surprising result → shareable. **Result presentation:** "You're leaving $45,230 on the table 💸" beats "Result: $45,230". Add comparisons ("compare with average/friends", percentiles).

### Validation checks
Missing social meta tags (add dynamic og:image/title/description per result) · non-deterministic results (use seeded/hash selection, not Math.random) · no share functionality · no shareable result image · desktop-first design (make mobile-first).

## 7. Marketing Ideas & Feasibility Scoring

**MFS = (Impact + Fit + Speed) − (Effort + Cost)** (−7 → +13). 10–13 do now · 7–9 prioritize · 4–6 test selectively · 1–3 defer · ≤0 don't recommend. Never recommend more than 5 ideas; never MFS ≤0; prefer high-signal, low-effort tests first.

**Stage bias:** pre-launch → speed > impact (waitlists, early access, content, communities) · early → speed + cost sensitivity (SEO, founder-led distribution, comparisons) · growth → impact (paid acquisition, partnerships, PLG loops) · scale → impact + defensibility (brand, international).

**Guardrails:** no idea dumping, no unscored recommendations, bias toward learning velocity, prefer compounding channels.

## 8. Growth Engine Playbook

A compact cross-domain operating checklist for a digital product:
1. **Audit** the full funnel (AARRR) with targets per stage.
2. **Fix activation first** — nothing scales without time-to-value (see CRO onboarding).
3. **SEO** landing page checklist: unique title/description, OG tags, SoftwareApplication schema, keywords split by intent (high-intent / informational / long-tail).
4. **Owned channels** (email, blog, community) as the base; rented and borrowed feed them.
5. **Viral loops + referral** with an explicit K-factor target and share moments designed into the product.
6. **Launch** in phases with Product Hunt preparation and post-launch momentum.
7. **Measure** activation, retention cohorts, revenue conversion, and referral rate; iterate.
